How we implement Grok Bot

Each phase ends with something your team can inspect. Timing depends on access and reviewer availability.

  1. Phase 01

    Map the intake desk

    The coordinator selects representative requests, including ambiguous and urgent ones. We document the queues, priority rules, and cases a bot must always escalate.

    You keep   A routing rubric and a labelled comparison set.

  2. Phase 02

    Isolate the pilot workspace

    With IT, verify plan access and set up a pilot bot with a read-only sample queue. Confirm it cannot assign tickets, notify customers, or access unrelated queues.

    You keep   A permission record and a witnessed no-write check.

  3. Phase 03

    Build the triage worksheet

    For each request, capture its ID, proposed queue, priority, reason, and missing evidence. A case matching two rules goes to coordinator review rather than the first matching queue.

    You keep   A standard recommendation sheet and an exception lane.

  4. Phase 04

    Shadow the coordinator

    Compare bot recommendations with human decisions on the same cases. Review urgent misses separately from routine disagreement. Do not widen access to fix a classification error.

    You keep   A scored trial with disagreement reasons and agreed release criteria.

  5. Phase 05

    Release in advisory mode

    The coordinator remains the only person assigning work. Review overrides at the agreed interval; suspend the bot and return to manual triage if it misses a critical escalation.

    You keep   An operator guide, escalation owner, and tested stop procedure.
