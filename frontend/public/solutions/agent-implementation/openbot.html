How we implement OpenBot

Each phase ends with something your team can inspect. Timing depends on access and reviewer availability.

  1. Phase 01

    Choose the handoff

    Trace one previous launch with the product and communications leads. Identify where a claim lost its source or an editor had to repeat research.

    You keep   A map of the current handoff, baseline editing time, and a named approver.

  2. Phase 02

    Give each coworker a job

    Agree workspace and access requirements with IT. The researcher reads approved release notes; the writer receives a fact sheet and a house style guide. Neither gets publishing credentials.

    You keep   Two role descriptions and a permission checklist.

  3. Phase 03

    Define the handoff

    For each proposed claim, the researcher records the source, release status, and unresolved questions. The writer drafts from confirmed claims and returns gaps instead of filling them in.

    You keep   A fact-sheet template, a drafting brief, and a worked example.

  4. Phase 04

    Rehearse a correction

    Replay a launch with a withdrawn feature. Check that the correction reaches the writer, the unsupported claim disappears, and the lead can trace every remaining claim before signing off.

    You keep   A reviewed example, exception log, and explicit release criteria.

  5. Phase 05

    Run one launch in parallel

    Keep the existing publishing process. Compare editing time and factual corrections, then let the launch lead decide whether to repeat the pilot. Practice pausing the handoff and reverting to manual drafting.

    You keep   An operator playbook, correction procedure, and expand-or-stop decision.
