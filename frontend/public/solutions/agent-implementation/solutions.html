The engagement

What changes hands

  1. Choose the job. You get a scope, a baseline, and a named reviewer.
  2. Build and test. We configure the workflow and test it on agreed cases, including exceptions.
  3. Hand it over. Your operator gets a walkthrough, a review checklist, and a way to stop the pilot.
